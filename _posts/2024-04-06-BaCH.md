Beatrice and Croc Harry

Questions and notes:

Why does the title only feature Croc Harry if Horace came with them too? He is also the third human!

Where did the giant come from? Is “giant” a way to describe or literal?

The story still has a lot of incomplete parts. Like what happened to the giant and what Beatrice will do with her family. I hope this is leaving an opportunity for a second book.

Characters:

Croc Harry:
Croc Harry has and his human form seems so different. They have the same personality. But one of them is loyal and the one of them is violent and hateful. This shows that Croc Harry is not a bad person naturally. I wonder why the queen does not allow a second chance to test. How come Croc Harry knows how to control a crocodile body and even things like Crocs honor? Does he have the memory of an existing crocodile?
