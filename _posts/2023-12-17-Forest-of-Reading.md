There’s a program called the Forest of Reading, that encourages students like me to read. The best books of the year are nominated by the program for a prize. These books sometimes get sent out to schools for the students to read and be the first to rate them. At the end of the year, a big festival is held where they announce the winner by popularity. I’ve decided to write a few reviews and analyses on the books that I’ve read. I’ve written notes for most of the books from 2024 and 2025, and I’ll post them here as I go.
