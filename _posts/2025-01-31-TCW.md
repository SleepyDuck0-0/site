The Cricket War

Notes:

This is a book based on true events, unlike the other books that were mainly fiction. That means conflict isn't as emotional, and the story doesn't have a clean hero's journey plot.

I felt like some of the decisions Tho made were weird. Why did he decide to leave on the pirate ship without at least telling Mai? He seems to value his friends and family, wanting to return to familiar people. I think it would have been safe to tell Mai the plan, so that he might have a friend on the way.

Characters:

Tho:
He went through a lot during his journey, but his story didn't strike me as much as some other refugee stories, such as “A Long Walk to Water” and “Refugee”. Those two books showed people the most affected by the conflict, those who are constantly suffering. Still, this book's writing style was more interesting. The thoughts and subconscious actions Tho has all look like a realistic child’s. The tiny things that reminded me about Tho’s character made the reading very immersive. It was also written in present tense, which is very rare.
