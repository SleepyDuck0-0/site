The Last Saxon King

Questions and Notes:

The author uses “he/him” for Sam before she is found out to be a girl. I noticed how she was assumed to be male.

Dad dying is kinda cliche. Dan knows that days in the present past normally while people are in the past. So why did he hope that his dad was alive? His reaction was not very emotional. 

The false alarm about William being dead was too short. It was just to mislead you for a tiny moment.


Characters:

Dan:
I feel sad for him being on the site of the battle that had to lose. He had to let his friends die. He is very loyal, shown by how he fought with his companions instead of fixing history and making sure they lose. A surprising number of people guessed the lies he told to blend in.

Sam:
Why does Sam bother with time jumping? Someone else usually fixes it. Is it to escape the real world? A problem with first person perspective is that you can't find out much about other characters. Why did Sam leave Dan alone at the start? Maybe she thought Dan was faking?
