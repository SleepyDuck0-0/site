When you picture a vacation, you usually think of something relaxing. But I think vacation is all about adventure. Sure, being taken away from the stress of work and your responsibilities makes it easier on yourself, but a truly great vacation is more than just relaxing. People go to places they’ve never been to and try new things because exploring is great. That's the kind of trip we’ll be going on in a few days when we drive to New York, Philadelphia, and Washington, D.C.

One thing I'm really looking forward to is taking pictures with my camera. I got it a few years ago from my grandpa, but the interface was in Chinese. Recently, I switched it to English and discovered a bunch of techniques I didn't know about. Hopefully, I will improve my photography skills and capture some nice moments in new places.

Going to a big city for a vacation is unusual compared to the tropical places we usually visit. One difference is we can go to places like museums. We don't have many museums around Toronto, so it will be great to see some famous ones who have more special exhibits.

In cities like New York and Washington, D.C., there are so many places to shop. That means there are shops for everyone! Shopping in such big cities is something unique that place has to offer. They have unique food, too. For example, I'm definitely anticipating trying an authentic New York street hot dog.

With so many places to explore, this vacation is sure to be interesting and full of adventure.
