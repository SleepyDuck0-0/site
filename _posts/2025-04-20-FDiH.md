40 Days In Hicksville

Notes:

This book has a weird approach to detective stories. For example, many books, like the Harry Potter series, have small clues from everyday life leading to a big reveal. This book, however, had chronological events that led to the next, and an unsatisfying story that was revealed slowly.

It seemed like the author didn't know much about the lives of children.

The style of writing is similar to Gordon Korman's with different perspectives, but Kate and Zack didn't have distinguishing personalities or opinions.


Characters:

Kate:
I like the idea of sadly and slowly revealing more about a person's life, but it didn't work with Kate's past. The emotions she felt weren’t clear.
