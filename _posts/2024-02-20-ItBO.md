Into the Bright Open

Questions and notes:

I noticed how different Mary is at the start compared to the end. Mary's parents were the main reason Mary's life was so Bland. They never encouraged her to try hard to solve problems, even though that's probably the way they become successful. Mary's parents let Miss Patricks take care of Mary instead of doing it themselves, making Mary not have the support and guidance of a family.

Characters:

Mary:
Mary discovered her true adventurous nature, which was pushed away at first by her parents. I connected this to the quote “Sometimes, even in darkness, if you push hard enough, you can end up sailing into the bright open.”

Sophie:
Mary and Sophie are like opposites. Mary was brought up in an orderly and controlled household and taught to respect rules, while Sophie was taught to be wild and explore. These two compliment each other well.

Flora:
Flora guided Mary through her first days at the manor, introducing her to a different world. Flora was very patient with Mary. I think that might have come from Flora being the oldest sibling in her family and taking care of her siblings before.
