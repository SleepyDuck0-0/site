Autumn Bird & the Runaway

Characters:

Autumn:
When Autumn got the opportunity to be popular, she took it. That greed made her leave her friends, and her new friends. And her new friends were disloyal and mean. This shows that you need to know whether you have true friends.

Cody:
Cody's dad never taught him to be a good person. He even influenced him with bad practices. I'm surprised Cody didn't report his abusive parents to the police. Cody didn't really do anything wrong in this book.

Cody’s Dad:
Cody’s dad takes his anger out on him. So I wonder if he was an abusive husband as well. Maybe his anger issue is what caused Cody's mom to fight with him? Maybe Cody's mom stealing might also be related to that as well.
